TASK 4 — HYPOTHESIS TESTING SUMMARY

Business Question:
Do average transaction sales differ between Male and Female customers?

Null Hypothesis (H0):
There is no statistically significant difference in average Total_Sales
between Male and Female customers.

Alternative Hypothesis (H1):
There is a statistically significant difference in average Total_Sales
between Male and Female customers.

Test:
Welch's independent two-sample t-test

Significance level:
alpha = 0.05

Results:
Male transactions: 511
Female transactions: 489
Male mean sales: ₹141,807.34
Female mean sales: ₹136,883.21
Mean difference (Male - Female): ₹4,924.13
t-statistic: 0.6826
p-value: 0.4950
Welch degrees of freedom: 997.98
95% confidence interval: ₹-9,231.58 to ₹19,079.85
Cohen's d: 0.0431

Decision:
Do not reject H0

Business Conclusion:
The analysis does not provide sufficient statistical evidence at the 5% significance level to conclude that average transaction sales differ between Male and Female customers.

Important interpretation:
The observed difference in sample means does not by itself establish
that gender causes differences in sales. This is an observational dataset,
so the result should be interpreted as an association within this sample.
